# Shoreline Change and Sea-Level Variation Along the Indian Coast

## GIS and DSAS-Based Coastal Change Analysis

This project investigates shoreline dynamics and mean sea-level variation along selected coastal regions of India using Geographic Information Systems (GIS), the Digital Shoreline Analysis System (DSAS), and long-term tide-gauge observations from the Permanent Service for Mean Sea Level (PSMSL).

The analysis focuses on selected coastal areas of **Chennai and Mumbai**, examining historical shoreline change, erosion and accretion patterns, Net Shoreline Movement (NSM), and temporal variation in mean sea level.

---

## Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

* Analyse historical shoreline changes along selected coastal regions of India.
* Identify areas experiencing shoreline erosion and accretion.
* Quantify shoreline movement using DSAS-derived measurements.
* Analyse spatial patterns of shoreline change using ArcGIS.
* Examine temporal variation in mean sea level using PSMSL tide-gauge observations.
* Compare observed sea-level trends with previously published studies.
* Assess patterns of coastal change in relation to long-term sea-level variation.

# Results

## Chennai

The Chennai mean-sea-level record showed relatively limited variation around an average level of approximately **7.026 m during 1980–2017**, followed by a more pronounced increase after 2017.

Between 1980 and 2022, the analysed MSL increased by approximately **0.297 m**, corresponding to an estimated rate of approximately **6.90 mm/year**.

The post-2017 increase represents a notable change in the observed time series. The causes of this variation require consideration of multiple local and regional factors and should not be attributed to a single factor without additional analysis.

## Mumbai

Mumbai showed considerable year-to-year variability in mean sea level during 1980–2022.

The analysed MSL increased from approximately **7.025 m in 1980** to approximately **7.157 m in 2022**, representing a net increase of approximately **0.132 m**.

The estimated rate of change from the analysis was approximately **3.06 mm/year**.
